EVIDENCE TO CAPTURE
Per channel: eligible visitors/devices, successful plan creators, voters, plans
receiving a vote from a different device, directions taps, time and cash spent.
Device IDs are browser proxies, not verified unique humans; cross-browser use,
shared devices, resets and manipulation limit the count. Confirm real participant
counts separately through observations; label assistance and exclude team tests.
Use source tags such as instagram, community, partner and field; internal QA must
be tagged separately. A directions click does not prove attendance or a booking.
There is no known winning user threshold. Aim first for several complete real
group journeys, then repeat the channel that produces them most efficiently.
Measure first group decision time against their existing group-chat process when
feasible; a few observations are learning evidence, not a statistical claim.
